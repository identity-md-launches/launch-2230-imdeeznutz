01 · Mechanics

The flywheel

1. Trade

Every swap in the $DEEZ/$IMD Uniswap v4 pool passes through the DEEZ hook. The hook takes exactly 2% of the $IMD side: from what you pay on a buy, from what you receive on a sell. The $DEEZ token itself is a plain ERC-20 with no transfer tax.

2. Accumulate

The fee is held inside the PoolManager as an ERC-6909 claim, recorded against the UTC day it was traded in. Anyone can call harvest() on the hook to move the accumulated $IMD into the rewards contract; each day's fees land in that day's pot, whenever the harvest happens.

3. Weigh

At the end of each UTC day the token's own balance history fixes each wallet's average balance over the day. The pool, the burn address and the protocol contracts are excluded; every other wallet counts, with no top-N cutoff.

4. Claim

Your share of a day's pot is pot × your average balance ÷ eligible supply. You claim your own share from your own wallet; the $IMD goes to you. A claim harvests the day's still-pending fees first, so you are paid your share of the whole day whenever you claim. Shares unclaimed for 90 days are recycled into the current pot.

02 · Rewards

The math, verifiable on chain

epoch            = floor(timestamp / 86400)
avg(who, e)      = average balance of who over day e   // from the token's own history
eligible(e)      = totalSupply
                   − avg(PoolManager, e)
                   − avg(0x…dEaD, e)
                   − avg(hook, e)
                   − avg(rewards, e)
share(you, e)    = pot(e) · avg(you, e) / eligible(e)

Every number is a public read: avg is DeezRewards.weightOf(who, e) (from DeezToken.getCumulativeBalanceOf), pot(e) is DeezRewards.pots(e), and DeezRewards.claimable(you, e) returns the share directly. Fees are credited to the day they were traded in, and a wallet that holds for one block of a day earns one block's worth of that day.

03 · Claim

Claim your $IMD

Connect a wallet on Ethereum mainnet to check the last 90 epochs.

Without this page

  1. Open the rewards contract on Etherscan and read currentEpoch().
  2. For any ended epoch, read claimable(yourAddress, epoch).
  3. Write claim(yourAddress, [epochs…]) from that same wallet; the $IMD is sent to yourAddress.
  4. No need to harvest first: claimable already counts the day's fees still held by the hook, and claim harvests them before paying. harvest() on the hook remains open to anyone who wants to move today's and yesterday's fees into their pots early.

05 · Supply & liquidity

Where the supply went

98%980,000,000 $DEEZ sent to DeezLiquidityLock and locked single-sided just above the opening price, in the launch transaction. The lock owns the position; it has no remove function and no owner.
1%10,000,000 $DEEZ of the requester's 2% sent to DeezLiquidityLock and locked the same way, in the same transaction.
1%10,000,000 $DEEZ, the rest of the requester's 2%, transferred to the requester wallet in the same transaction.

Circulating supply is everything outside the pool and the lock. Tokens inside the pool earn nothing; every other wallet earns in proportion to its balance. The lock's status (totalLocked0, totalLocked1) is public on the lock contract.
